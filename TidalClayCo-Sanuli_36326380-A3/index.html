Our Story

Made by hand, inspired by the coast

Every piece of Tidal Clay Co. pottery is handcrafted in Western Australia, inspired by the quiet beauty of the Australian coast.

Our ceramics are designed to bring simple beauty into everyday moments. Organic shapes, subtle colours and natural textures reflect the rugged yet delicate character of coastal living.

We use carefully selected Australian clays and food-safe, non-toxic glazes to create pieces that are beautiful, practical and made to be enjoyed every day.
